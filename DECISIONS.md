# Decisions

Newest first. If this file conflicts with earlier chat, this file wins.

## 2026-10-03

1. The first screen lets the person choose between "Start with my income and goals" and "Start with my bank file".
2. Most transactions come in by importing a bank CSV.
3. Opening a fund shows the last 12 months first (what has been saved, or how far over budget), with a Month view one tap away.
4. Making a fund is a short guided wizard, one question per screen.
5. First-time setup is guided, 5 to 7 steps, and each step explains why it is asking.
6. Money that is set aside and keeps what you don't spend is called a Fund. The word "bucket" goes away in everything the person sees.
7. Main navigation is: Home, Budget, Funds, Grow, Account. Funds has its own tab.
8. Only the Nerd analytics tools stay hidden until asked for. Payback matching, splitting a charge, merchant rules, and the year spreadsheet and charts stay visible.
9. For funds, "the year" means the last 12 months, always rolling.
